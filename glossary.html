Accessibility Glossary

What is Visual Accessibility?
Visual accessibility ensures that digital content can be used by people with low vision, dyslexia, color blindness, and other visual processing needs.

Why It Matters:

  • Improves readability for millions of users
  • Supports people with dyslexia through specialized fonts
  • Helps color-blind users interpret information correctly
  • Reduces eye strain with low-brightness modes
  • Creates inclusive digital environments

Who Benefits?
People with low vision, dyslexia, ADHD, migraines, color blindness, and even users in bright sunlight or low-light environments.
